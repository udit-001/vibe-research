# Question Templates for Different Research Types

Use these templates to guide your questioning during the iterative refinement phase. Pick the section that matches the user's research domain.

---

## Technology Comparisons

- What specific use case is this technology for?
- What are your must-have vs nice-to-have features?
- What's your timeline for evaluation and decision?

---

## Library Evaluation

- What problem are you trying to solve with this library?
- What's your current stack and integration requirements?
- What level of support/maintenance do you require?

---

## Debugging Research

- What's the exact error message and context?
- When does this occur (consistently, intermittently, under specific conditions)?
- What have you already tried to resolve it?

---

## Market/Business Research

- What market, industry, or business area are you exploring?
- What decision will this research inform? (entering market, pricing strategy, product positioning)
- Who is your target audience or customer segment?
- What geographic scope and timeframe are relevant?

---

## Competitive Analysis

- Who are your main competitors or alternatives you're evaluating?
- What dimensions matter most? (pricing, features, market share, customer satisfaction)
- Are you looking at direct competitors or adjacent/alternative solutions?
- What's your competitive advantage or unique positioning?

---

## Content/Topic Research

- What topic or subject area do you need to understand?
- What's the purpose? (writing content, preparing a presentation, learning a subject)
- What depth do you need? (overview, detailed analysis, expert-level)
- Who is the intended audience for the final output?

---

## Process/Workflow Evaluation

- What current process or workflow are you looking to improve?
- What pain points or inefficiencies are you experiencing?
- What constraints exist? (budget, time, team size, existing tools)
- What does success look like for the improved process?

---

## General Decision Support

- What are the specific options or alternatives you're considering?
- What criteria matter most for this decision? (cost, speed, quality, risk)
- What's your timeline for making this decision?
- Who else needs to be involved or informed?
